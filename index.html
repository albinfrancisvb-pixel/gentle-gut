          Brave steps 🧗

          IBS can make us avoid things we used to enjoy. Facing them in small, safe steps teaches your brain that it's okay again.

          How it works

          1. Pick one thing IBS made you avoid.
          2. Start with the easiest step. Feeling nervous is normal, and it passes.
          3. Repeat it until it feels okay, then move up a step.

          Start a new ladder

          You're going to be okay 💚
